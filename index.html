How this is calculated
  1. Annual team hours searching team members × minutes per day ÷ 60 × working days
  2. Hourly employee cost from the pay you enter: daily ÷ 8, monthly × 12 ÷ (working days × 8) or yearly ÷ (working days × 8).
  3. Estimated annual time cost annual hours × hourly employee cost
  4. Equivalent working days annual hours ÷ 8, counted as person-days across the whole team.
  5. Hypothetical scenario annual hours × your reduction and annual time cost × your reduction
  6. AI token savings monthly token spend × your token saving, and × 12 for a year.

The calculator keeps full precision and rounds only for display. It runs in your browser and does not send or store what you enter.
